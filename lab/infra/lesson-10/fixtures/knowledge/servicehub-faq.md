# ServiceHub FAQ (fictional lab fixture)

Contoso ServiceHub schedules and tracks field maintenance for customer equipment.

- **How do I book a maintenance visit?** Open ServiceHub, choose the site, and pick a slot in the next 14 days.
- **What does a standard visit include?** An inspection, firmware checks, and a written report within one business day.
- **Who approves emergency visits?** The on-call service operations engineer.

This file is part of the SC-500 Lesson 10 lab. It contains no real customer data.
