## 90-day deep dive (about 3 hours per week)

Spread the 60-day plan over 12 weeks instead of 8, and add a dedicated week for each of:

- **Week 13:** Cross-lesson integration lab — production-style hub-and-spoke with Defender, Sentinel, and Copilot wired end-to-end
- **Week 14:** Two timed MeasureUp practice exams with full review of every miss

---

## The three pillars (Tim's rule)

Every study week, touch all three:

1. **Theoretical knowledge** — video lessons, Microsoft Learn modules
2. **Hands-on experience** — demo scripts in this repo, your own sandbox
3. **Practice exam review** — MeasureUp scored attempts with debrief

Skip any one pillar and your retention curve falls off. Done is better than perfect, but balance is non-negotiable.
