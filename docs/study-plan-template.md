# Study plan template

Three schedules, each about 40 hours in total. Pick the one that fits your calendar, copy it into your planner, and protect the time.

## 30-day sprint (4 weeks, about 10 hours per week)

| Week | Lessons | Focus |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 1, 2, 3 | Identity, governance, and Key Vault. Stand up a sandbox subscription with a budget alert. |
| 2 | 4, 5, 6, 7 | Storage, databases, and networking. Build a small hub-and-spoke network. |
| 3 | 8, 9, 10, 11 | Servers, application platforms, and AI workloads. Onboard a VM and create a Foundry project. |
| 4 | 12, 13, 14, 15 | Posture, Microsoft Sentinel, and Security Copilot. Take timed practice questions and schedule your exam. |

## 60-day plan (8 weeks, about 5 hours per week)

| Week | Lessons | Focus |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 1, 2 | Microsoft Entra identity and access end to end |
| 2 | 3 | Key Vault, Azure Policy, compliance, backup, and infrastructure as code |
| 3 | 4 | Storage and database security |
| 4 | 5, 6, 7 | Segmentation, private connectivity, and the perimeter |
| 5 | 8, 9 | Servers, VMs, and application platform services |
| 6 | 10, 11 | AI workload protection |
| 7 | 12, 13 | Defender for Cloud posture and Microsoft Sentinel ingestion |
| 8 | 14, 15 | Sentinel analytics and automation, Security Copilot, and timed practice questions |

## 90-day deep dive (13 weeks, about 3 hours per week)

Spread the 60-day plan over the first 11 weeks, one or two lessons per week, then add:

- **Week 12:** A cross-lesson lab. Wire a hub-and-spoke network, Defender for Cloud, Microsoft Sentinel, and Security Copilot together and trace one incident from alert to response.
- **Week 13:** Two timed practice exams, with a full review of every miss against the [exam objective map](exam-objectives.md).

## The three pillars

Every study week, touch all three:

1. **Theory:** the video lessons, Microsoft Learn, and the official study guide
2. **Hands-on experience:** the walkthroughs in this repository, in your own sandbox
3. **Practice questions:** timed attempts with a debrief of every miss. Microsoft's free practice assessment for SC-500 was not available when this page was checked on November 8, 2026, so check the [certification page](https://learn.microsoft.com/credentials/certifications/cloud-and-ai-security-engineer-associate/).

Skip a pillar and your retention falls off. Done beats perfect, but balance is not optional.
